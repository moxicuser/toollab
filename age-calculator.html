How to use the age calculator

  1. Select your birth date from the date field.
  2. Choose Calculate age to compare it with today's date.
  3. Read your age in years, months, and days, along with the countdown to your next birthday.

How age is calculated

The calculator compares your birth date with today's local calendar date. It first counts full years, then full months since your most recent birthday, and finally the remaining days. When the current day number is earlier than your birth day, it borrows the number of days in the previous calendar month. This accounts for months having different lengths and for leap years.

Your next birthday is found by checking whether this year's birthday has passed. If it has, the calculator uses next year's date and counts the calendar days remaining.

Example

If a person was born on May 10, 2000 and today is October 2, 2026, the result is 26 years, 4 months, and 22 days. Their next birthday is 220 days away. The exact result updates automatically based on the date you use the calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Does it calculate age as of today?

Yes. It uses the current date on your device when you calculate.

How are leap-day birthdays handled?

For the birthday countdown, a February 29 birthday is observed on February 28 in years without a leap day.

Is my birth date saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and the date is not sent to a server.

Can I calculate an age in the future?

No. A birth date later than today is not accepted as a valid age.
