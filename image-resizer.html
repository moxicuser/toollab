How to use the image resizer

  1. Select a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 20 MB.
  2. Enter the target width and height in pixels.
  3. Leave Keep aspect ratio selected to update the other dimension proportionally.
  4. Choose an output format, resize, inspect the preview, and download the new image.

How image resizing works

Resizing draws the source image onto a new canvas with the requested pixel dimensions. Keeping the aspect ratio uses the source width-to-height ratio to calculate the other side, which avoids stretching. Turning it off lets you set both dimensions independently but may distort the image. Enlarging a small image cannot restore detail that was not present in the source and may make it look soft.

Choose dimensions for your use

Smaller pixel dimensions can reduce download size and fit thumbnails or profile images. Keep a copy of the original before resizing. The tool processes the image in your browser, and the selected file is not uploaded to ToolLab.

Frequently asked questions

Does resizing change image quality?

Changing dimensions can remove detail or soften an image. JPEG and WebP encoding quality also affects the saved result.

What does keep aspect ratio do?

It preserves the source proportions by automatically adjusting the other dimension when you edit width or height.

Can I enlarge an image?

Yes, but enlarging cannot create source detail and may make the image look less sharp.

Are images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens locally in your browser.
