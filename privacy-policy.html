Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use ToolLab. ToolLab is a collection of browser-based utilities. The site does not currently require accounts.

Files and information you enter

The age calculator, date calculator, unit converter, QR generator, image compressor, image resizer, and file converter process their inputs in your browser. ToolLab does not receive the files or values used by these tools. The QR generator loads its QR-code library from a public CDN; the file converter loads conversion libraries from public CDNs. Those providers receive ordinary network request information when your browser downloads a library, but not the selected file contents through the tool's processing.

The currency converter sends the selected base currency to ExchangeRate-API Open Access to request rates. The amount you enter is applied in your browser and is not included in that rates request.

Hosting and service providers

ToolLab is hosted on Vercel. Hosting providers may process standard request and security information such as IP address, requested page, browser details, timestamps, and error data to deliver and protect the site. Google Fonts and the third-party libraries and rate provider described above are subject to their own privacy policies and practices.

Cookies and advertising

ToolLab does not currently run advertising or include an advertising network or analytics tracker in these pages. The site itself does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Vercel or linked third-party services may use technical storage or cookies under their own policies.

If advertising such as Google AdSense is added later, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to store preferences, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, or personalize ads where permitted. This policy will be updated before advertising is enabled, and consent controls will be provided where required by law.

Retention and choices

ToolLab does not store the files processed by its browser tools. Third-party providers may retain their own service logs according to their policies. You can choose not to use a tool that requires an external rate or library request, and you can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings.

Children and policy updates

ToolLab is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect personal information from children. This policy may change when tools or third-party services change. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. Do not post sensitive personal information in the public GitHub issue form.

This policy is a plain-language description of the current site, not legal advice or a guarantee of approval by any advertising program.
