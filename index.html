How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Choose the currency you are converting from and the currency you want.
  3. Press Convert to request the latest available reference rate.
  4. Use the swap control to reverse the currency pair.

How exchange conversion works

The converter multiplies your amount by the exchange rate for the selected currency pair. Rates come from the ExchangeRate-API Open Access feed, which updates daily. These are indicative rates, not guaranteed rates for a transaction; banks and card providers may add a spread or fee, and market rates can move after the published update.

Converted amount = amount × exchange rate

For example, if 1 USD is worth 0.92 EUR, then 100 USD converts to 92 EUR before any provider fees. The rate date appears below the result when a conversion succeeds.

Reference rates, not transaction quotes

This free tool is useful for travel planning, shopping comparisons, and quick estimates. It requires an internet connection to retrieve rates. It does not exchange money or store the amounts you enter. Always check the final rate and fees with your bank or payment provider before sending or spending money.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Rates are retrieved from ExchangeRate-API Open Access and are refreshed daily.

Are these live trading rates?

No. They are daily reference rates, not real-time quotes or guaranteed rates from a bank.

Why might my bank show a different amount?

Your bank or payment service may use a different rate and charge a conversion fee or spread.

Does the converter save my amount?

No. The amount is used in your browser and is not stored by this page.
